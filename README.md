AI Customer Support Assistant

Project Overview

This project demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can help businesses improve customer service and respond to common customer enquiries.

Project Objective

To demonstrate how an AI-powered assistant can:

• Answer frequently asked customer questions.
• Provide basic product and service information.
• Help businesses save time.
• Improve customer communication.

Tools and Technologies

• ChatGPT
• AI prompting
• Business process automation

Example Customer Interaction

Customer: What are your business opening hours?

AI Assistant: Thank you for contacting us. Our business hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM.

Skills Demonstrated

• AI tools
• Prompt engineering
• Customer support
• Business automation
• Problem-solving

Project Status

Beginner portfolio project. More features will be added as the project develops.
